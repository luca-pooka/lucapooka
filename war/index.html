Rules

Both players will choose between four moves each round, choosing from:

  • 🔫 (shoot) — Shoots the other player
  • 🪞 (reflect) — If the other player shoots, this reflects the bullet back at them
  • 🔁 (reload) — Reloads your gun; gives you one ammo
  • 🛡️ (shield) — Blocks a player's bullet

Both 🔫 and 🪞 cost one piece of ammo to use.
